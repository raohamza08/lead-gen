# AI-Powered Revenue Intelligence & Client Acquisition Operating System
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

---

# Overview

## Vision

Build a production-ready AI-powered Revenue Intelligence Platform that functions as an autonomous AI Sales Department rather than a traditional lead generation tool.

The platform should automate nearly the entire outbound sales lifecycle, allowing humans to focus only on strategy, approval, relationship building, and closing deals.

---

# Core Objective

Create an internal SaaS platform capable of:

- Discovering potential customers
- Understanding each business
- Identifying operational pain points
- Detecting AI and automation opportunities
- Recommending services
- Personalizing outreach
- Managing follow-ups
- Tracking engagement
- Predicting buying probability
- Continuously improving through feedback

---

# Human Responsibilities

Users should only:

- Define target market
- Configure filters
- Review AI recommendations
- Approve outreach
- Conduct sales conversations
- Close deals

Everything else should be fully automated.

---
