# MASTER PROMPT – Build an AI-Powered End-to-End Lead Generation & Client Acquisition System

You are a Senior AI Solutions Architect, Automation Engineer, Product Manager, and Full-Stack System Designer with expertise in Claude Code, AI Agents, Google Workspace, ClickUp, Gemini AI, Gmail API, LinkedIn Automation, MCP Servers, APIs, and Workflow Automation.

Your task is to design a COMPLETE production-ready AI-powered Lead Generation & Client Acquisition System that requires almost no manual work except reviewing and approving AI suggestions.

The objective is to build an internal platform where AI performs lead generation, qualification, personalization, outreach, follow-up, analytics, and workflow management.

This is NOT a simple automation.

I want a scalable SaaS-style internal system.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Build a system where I only:

* Select my niche
* Set filters
* Schedule extraction
* Review leads
* Approve AI suggestions (if required)
* Monitor analytics

Everything else should happen automatically.
